## Findings that change the design

- **The API returns JPEG.** Every result was `image/jpeg`. The server converts to PNG via sharp
  on the way to disk; nothing downstream should assume the API gives PNG.
- **Style references work on Flash in practice.** The docs list style slots as Pro-only, but
  attaching Morgash's token with "this is a style and camera reference, do not copy its subject"
  held the ink-line style and the top-down angle on Flash. Tokens stay on Flash.
- **Tokens are top-down full-body, not waist-up busts.** The world's tokens (e.g. `morgash.png`)
  are high three-quarter top-down full figures on transparency. The token preset attaches an
  existing world token as the angle and style reference.
- **Chroma phrasing must be explicit.** "plain flat solid bright green background" made Flash
  paint a green token *disc* on white. "The ENTIRE image background, edge to edge and corner to
  corner, is one flat uniform chroma-key green (#00FF00) with no circle, no disc, no ring, no
  border, no ground, no shadow" fixed it on both subjects tried. That sentence is the server's
  token suffix, not the skill's.
- **The molten5e chroma cutout works unchanged** on API output: 512 square, scale 1.0, clean
  edges on the explicit-green plates. The first wolf (less uniform green) left green patches
  between the legs, so rembg stays as the fallback method.
- **Refusals: none.** Blood spray, a corpse, arrows in flesh, a severed goblin head on a spike, a
  burning farmhouse, all rendered on both tiers. Treat the content filter as a non-issue for
  table art until proven otherwise.
- **Pro is worth it where it was predicted to be:** party scenes (identity fidelity) and
  illustrations (style refs). Flash at 1K is the right default for icons and tokens; nothing
  suggested an icon needs Pro.
- **Latency:** Flash ~8 s at 1K; Pro ~15 s at 1K, ~25 s at 2K; both tiers ~30 s for a 2K party
  scene with four references. Per-call timeout of 120 s is comfortable.
- **A signature scribble** appeared bottom-right on the Flash party scene. Same class of defect
  as the old FLUX ghost signatures; crop or re-roll, do not fight it in the prompt.

## Decisions taken

- Kinds and tiers stand as written in CLAUDE.md, with the token preset corrected to top-down
  full-body plus a world-token style reference.
- Illustrations at 2K were already good; 4K stays the preset for the 2560×1600 crop but is not
  required for quality.
- The style shelf for Pro is the three portraits Gren / Morgash / Thomas until illustrations are
  approved under the new backend.

Total estimated spend: **$3.79**.
